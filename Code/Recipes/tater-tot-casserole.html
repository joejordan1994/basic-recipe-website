Tater Tot Casserole

The best there ever was. The best there ever will be. Period.

Description

Tater Tot Casserole is comfort food at its finest—hearty, creamy, crispy, and nostalgic. Ground beef, sautéed aromatics, and mixed vegetables form the base, all folded into a rich, savory sauce. Topped with golden tater tots and gooey melted cheese, each bite brings warmth and satisfaction. It’s simple, familiar, and just plain delicious.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables
  • 1 can (10.5 oz) condensed cream of mushroom soup
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 bag (32 oz) frozen tater tots
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: fresh parsley or chives for garnish

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the beef in a skillet until browned. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Add onion and garlic, cooking until soft and fragrant (3–5 minutes).
  4. Stir in veggies and cook for 2–3 more minutes. Remove from heat.
  5. Mix sauce: Combine mushroom soup and sour cream in a bowl.
  6. Combine sauce with meat/veggie mix and stir to coat evenly.
  7. Layer in dish: Meat mixture first, followed by 1 cup of cheese.
  8. Top with tots in a single neat layer.
  9. Bake for 25 minutes or until tots are crisp and golden.
  10. Add cheese: Sprinkle remaining cheese on top, bake 5 more minutes.
  11. Garnish with herbs if desired. Let cool slightly.
  12. Serve warm and enjoy!
