Breakfast Tacos

The best breakfast there ever was, the best breakfast there ever will be.

Description

Breakfast tacos are a morning masterpiece: fluffy eggs, crispy bacon or chorizo, creamy avocado, and melty cheese all wrapped in a warm tortilla. Finished with fresh cilantro, salsa, and a hint of sour cream—it's the kind of breakfast that turns any day into a good one.

Ingredients

  • 4–6 soft flour or corn tortillas
  • 6 eggs, beaten
  • ½ pound cooked and crumbled bacon
  • 1 sliced avocado
  • ½ cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • Fresh cilantro, finely chopped
  • Salsa verde
  • Sour cream
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Butter (for cooking eggs)

Steps

  1. Cook the bacon until crisp. Drain, then crumble or chop.
  2. Scramble the eggs over medium-low heat with butter. Cook until softly set.
  3. Warm tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave wrapped in a damp cloth.
  4. Fill tortillas with scrambled eggs.
  5. Add toppings: bacon/chorizo, avocado, and cheese.
  6. Garnish with sour cream, salsa, and cilantro or chives.
  7. Serve immediately and enjoy!
