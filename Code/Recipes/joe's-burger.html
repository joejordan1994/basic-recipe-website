Joe's Burger

The best burger there ever was, the best burger there ever will be.

Description

This isn’t just any burger—it’s a dinner-worthy showstopper. A juicy beef patty, seasoned and grilled to perfection, rests on a toasted brioche bun. Melted aged cheese brings richness, while fresh lettuce and ripe tomato deliver crisp, cool contrast. Finished with a swipe of gourmet sauce, every bite balances warmth and brightness, simplicity and indulgence. It’s not just dinner—it’s a moment.

Ingredients

  • 1 seasoned beef patty (salt + pepper)
  • 1 artisan brioche bun, halved and toasted
  • 1 slice aged cheese (cheddar or Gruyère)
  • 1 fresh lettuce leaf
  • 1 slice ripe tomato
  • Gourmet sauce (like aioli or burger sauce)
  • Butter (for toasting)

Steps

  1. Season the beef patty with salt and pepper.
  2. Cook on a grill or skillet over medium-high heat, 3–4 mins per side for medium-rare.
  3. Toast the bun in butter until golden while the patty cooks.
  4. Add cheese to the patty in the final minute to melt.
  5. Assemble: bun base → lettuce → tomato → cheesy patty.
  6. Top it off with sauce on the upper bun and close the burger.
  7. Serve hot and enjoy immediately!
